// 01 — WHAT IS THE MOTORSPORT ENGINEERING BOOTCAMP?

From your passion for racing to a technical understanding of the race car

// THE PROGRAMME

The Motorsport Engineering Bootcamp is an intensive online programme designed to build a solid foundation in Race Engineering.

You will explore vehicle dynamics, aerodynamics, data acquisition, race car setup and race weekend procedures, while learning the technical language and methodologies used in professional Motorsport.

// WHAT YOU WILL LEARN
  • How a professional Motorsport team operates
  • The roles of the Race Engineer, Track Engineer and Data Engineer
  • How to analyse race car behaviour
  • How to read and interpret data acquisition
  • How race car setup is developed
  • How suspension, tyres, brakes, aerodynamics and differential systems interact
  • How to prepare for and analyse a race weekend
  • Which software and methodologies are used in professional Motorsport

Build the right technical foundation

The Bootcamp does not require an entrance selection.

However, students should be aware that the programme covers technical and specialised subjects. Before enrolling, you should therefore assess your own background and your readiness to engage with the technical content of the course.

// THE FINAL EXAM

At the end of the Bootcamp, students will take a final examination consisting of:

  • 01 — Written test An assessment of the technical knowledge developed throughout the programme.
  • 02 — Technical & aptitude interview An interview designed to assess technical understanding and aptitude for further education in Motorsport Engineering.

Students who successfully pass the final examination will be eligible to access the selection process for the Master Race Car Engineer.
